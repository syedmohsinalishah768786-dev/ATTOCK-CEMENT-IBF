# ATTOCK-CEMENT-IBF: Instructions for Report and Presentation

## 1. Task
Create a **report** and a **presentation** for the Introduction to Business Finance (IBF) project: financial ratio analysis of **Attock Cement Pakistan Limited (PSX: ACPL)**.
- Period: [LAST 3 / 5 FISCAL YEARS]. Fiscal year ends 30 June.
- Report: [.docx / .pdf], maximum [N] pages. Presentation: .pptx, maximum [N] slides.

## 2. Scope rule (strict)
- The ratios, formulas and layout come ONLY from the course notes in `/notes/` (ratio table: debt, liquidity, profitability, efficiency, per-share, relative value).
- Do NOT add ratios, models or methods that are not in the notes (no extra ratios, no DuPont, no additional metrics).
- Where the notes' formula differs from other sources, the notes win. Use [360 / 365] days where a days-in-year figure is needed.
- Read the notes first and list the exact ratios you will compute before starting.

## 3. Data rules
- Use only Attock Cement's official annual and quarterly reports and PSX data (prices, share counts).
- Cite source and year for each figure. State units (PKR thousands or millions).
- Never invent numbers. If a figure is missing, write "Not available".
- Show formula, inputs and result for each ratio.

## 4. Ratios to compute (from the notes only)
| Group | Ratios |
|---|---|
| (a) Debt | Debt ratio, Leverage (total assets / total equity), Times interest earned |
| (b) Liquidity | Current ratio, Quick ratio |
| (c) Profitability | Net profit margin, ROA, ROE, Gross profit margin, Earning power |
| (d) Efficiency | Inventory turnover, Days sales outstanding, Asset turnover |
| (e) Per-share | EPS, Book value per share, Dividend per share |
| (f) Relative value | P/E, P/B |
Present in the notes' format: Ratio | Formula | Calculation | Result | Use. Add a one-line trend comment per ratio across the years.

## 5. Report structure (keep each section brief)
1. **Company introduction**: history, ownership, plants, products, capacity (half a page)
2. **Market**: PSX, the cement sector, and the company's position in it
3. **Shares in possession**: authorized/issued/paid-up capital, shares outstanding, shareholding pattern, price, market cap
4. **Competitors**: [N] listed cement companies; table of shares outstanding, price and market cap versus Attock Cement; short explanation of how the differences affect the trend and influence the company's market position
5. **Ratio analysis**: the tables from section 4 and trend comments
6. **Forecast**: next [2/3] years for revenue, net profit and EPS only, using simple trend/average growth with stated assumptions; label as illustrative, not guaranteed
7. **Recommendations**: for management and investors, each tied to a specific ratio result
8. **Conclusion and references**

## 6. Visuals (only for the stated ratios)
- One trend line chart per ratio group (a to f)
- Bar chart: shares outstanding and market cap, Attock Cement vs competitors
- Pie chart: shareholding pattern
- Forecast chart: historical plus projected revenue/net profit/EPS
Each chart needs a title, axis labels, units and a legend.

## 7. Presentation ([N] slides max)
Title; company intro; market; shares; competitors; one slide per ratio group (a to f) with table and chart; forecast; recommendations; conclusion. Minimal text; interpretation goes in speaker notes.

## 8. Style and output
Simple professional English, numbered tables and figures, title page, page numbers. Save to `report/`, `presentation/`, `data/` (Excel with all inputs and ratio calculations) and `charts/`. Do not exceed the stated length limits or the stated ratio list.
